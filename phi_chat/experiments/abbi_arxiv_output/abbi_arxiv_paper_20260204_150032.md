## Attention Formula
The attention mechanism is a key component of models like Transformers, enabling them to weigh the relevance of different input elements when generating output. The formula for computing attention is given by:
$$\text{Attention}(Q, K, V) = \text{softmax}\left(\frac{QK^T}{\sqrt{d}}\right)V$$
Here, $Q$ is the query matrix, $K$ is the key matrix, and $V$ is the value matrix. The dimensions of $Q$, $K$, and $V$ are typically equal, and $d$ is the dimensionality of these matrices. The division by $\sqrt{d}$ is used to normalize the dot product between query and key vectors, ensuring that the attention scores are not dominated by the magnitude of the vectors.
