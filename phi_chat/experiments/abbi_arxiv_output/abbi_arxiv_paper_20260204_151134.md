## φ-Level Definition
The φ-level is a measure of the information content within each layer of a Transformer network. It is defined as:
$$\phi\text{-level}_l = \frac{\log(\|h_l\|)}{\log \phi}$$
where $\|h_l\|$ represents the norm of the hidden state vector at layer $l$, and $\phi = \frac{1 + \sqrt{5}}{2}$ is the golden ratio. This logarithmic scaling ensures that the φ-level reflects the relative information content rather than absolute magnitude, which becomes crucial for understanding the flow of information throughout the network.

## Attention Formula
The attention mechanism in a Transformer network is given by:
$$\text{Attention}(Q,K,V) = \text{softmax}\left(\frac{QK^T}{\sqrt{d}}\right)V$$
Here, $Q$, $K$, and $V$ represent the query, key, and value matrices respectively, while $d$ denotes the dimensionality of the input vectors. The softmax function normalizes the dot product between the query and key vectors, scaled by $\sqrt{d}$, to ensure that the resulting probabilities sum up to one. This mechanism allows the network to weigh the relevance of different parts of the input sequence to each other, effectively enabling the model to focus on relevant information when generating outputs.

## Mathematical Formula for Style Transfer

The formula for applying style transfer involves adding a scaled style vector to the content vector:

$$\text{styled} = \text{content} + \lambda \cdot \text{style\_vector}$$

Here, `content` represents the original content vector, `style\_vector` is the vector representing the desired style, and `λ` is a scalar that adjusts the intensity of the style influence on the content. This equation allows for the manipulation of text in various styles by simply adjusting the `λ` parameter and the `style\_vector`.
