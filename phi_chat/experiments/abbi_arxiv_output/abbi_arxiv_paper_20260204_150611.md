### Attention Formula
The attention mechanism calculates the relevance of different parts of the input to the output. The core formula for attention is given by:
$$\text{Attention}(Q,K,V) = \text{softmax}\left(\frac{QK^T}{\sqrt{d}}\right)V$$
Here, $Q$ is the query matrix, $K$ is the key matrix, and $V$ is the value matrix. The dot product between $Q$ and $K$ is scaled by $\frac{1}{\sqrt{d}}$, where $d$ is typically the dimensionality of the keys. The resulting scaled dot products are then passed through the softmax function to obtain attention weights, which are then multiplied by the value matrix to produce the attended output.
