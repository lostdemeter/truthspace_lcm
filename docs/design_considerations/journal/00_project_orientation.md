# Project Orientation

## What This Archive Is

This journal is a curated reading layer over the raw design considerations archive.
It is meant to help a reader reconstruct the evolution of the project without losing the original sequence of observations.

## What This Project Is Trying to Show

The central project hypothesis is that intelligence in models is geometric.
Weights are not the irreducible source of knowledge. They are one way of storing and traversing structure.
The long-term ambition is to show that:
- structure is information
- geometry is computation
- learned models can be read as geometric objects
- parts of their behavior can be reconstructed without standard neural execution

## What Makes The Archive Hard To Read

The original DCs were usually written close to the moment of discovery.
That makes them valuable, but also creates several difficulties:
- repeated ideas across many files
- terminology drift over time
- local hypotheses later superseded by stronger formulations
- synthesis documents that depend on many earlier documents
- chronological order that is not yet editorially reconstructed

## What This Journal Preserves

This journal should preserve:
- observations as they first appeared
- failed or partial explanations
- moments of conceptual transition
- consolidation points where several lines of work converged
- explicit distinction between evidence and interpretation

## Status Vocabulary

Each journal entry should use the following statuses where possible:

- Observation
  - something directly seen in experiment or code behavior
- Hypothesis
  - a proposed explanation or extrapolation
- Validation
  - a claim supported by repeated or stronger evidence
- Current interpretation
  - the best present synthesis, which may still change

## Provenance Rule

Every nontrivial claim in this directory should be traceable back to source DCs.
When possible, the journal should prefer citing a small number of anchor documents rather than duplicating detail.

## Writing Style

The intended tone is closer to an explorer's field journal than a polished textbook.
The goal is not rhetorical certainty. The goal is to preserve the path by which the worldview was formed.
